When comparing grass-fed whey protein isolate to regular whey protein, it's crucial to recognize the fundamental difference: sourcing. Grass-fed whey comes from cows that graze on pasture, potentially leading to a cleaner, more natural product without artificial additives. In contrast, regular whey protein is often derived from more industrialized farming practices. This key distinction can affect not only the nutritional profile but also the overall quality of your protein supplement.
